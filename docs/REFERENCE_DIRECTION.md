# Reference Direction — Hybrid Premium V5

The references below are used as **principle sources**, never as layouts to copy.

## Core project references
- Casa Aurora / home-3d-three: spatial website as the primary interface; guided visual orientation; space is the story.
- ERA Residence: warm architectural materiality, large breathing room, image-first storytelling, restrained luxury.
- LPAS: explicit scroll/swipe invitation, human-centered architecture communication, strong content hierarchy.
- Senawa Studio: editorial project index, restrained typography, architectural detail as narrative, character/context/composition.
- Studio Foundry: restrained luxury, oversized typography, honest materials, project imagery as protagonist.

## Applied synthesis for Lamim's
- Keep the 3D environment as the memorable signature interaction.
- Scroll is the only spatial movement input on desktop, tablet and mobile.
- Preserve the dark charcoal/espresso frame from the first premium build, but use warm ivory/stone surfaces for content-heavy reading.
- Avoid black-on-black geometry and avoid generic SaaS card grids.
- Use typography, thin rules, numbered editorial markers, asymmetry and image scale to create luxury rather than excessive effects.
- Keep the barbershop human: team, reviews, services and physical atmosphere remain visible parts of the story.
- Use adaptive quality so the same art direction survives on mobile and lower-end devices.

## Non-negotiable interaction rules
- Clicking the initial CTA reveals the existing interior scene; it does not physically walk through the glass door.
- Scroll down advances the interior camera path; scroll up reverses it.
- Menus/drawers freeze the journey at its exact position and restore it without jumping.
- Reaching the final chair does not lock the document or replace the scene with a black fullscreen state.
- Mobile contains no movement joystick, WASD analogue or persistent movement buttons.
